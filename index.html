Inkognito

The word lies. Or does it?

The word says RED. The paint says BLUE. Which one do you trust?

Inkognito is a fast, fiendish colour game built on the Stroop effect, the quirk of your brain that makes reading and seeing fight each other. A switch tells you the rule for every card: tap the colour of the INK, or tap the colour the WORD spells. It flips at random, so you can never switch off.

Three ways to play

Daily. 15 cards, the same for everyone, one go per day. Share your time and a grid of coloured squares without spoiling a thing.

Sprint. 60 seconds, as many cards as you can.

Arcade. 3 lives and a ticking clock. Every level adds one twist, until even the buttons lie.

Good to know

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline. Available in English, Italian, Catalan, Spanish and French for iPhone and iPad.
