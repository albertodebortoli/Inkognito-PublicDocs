Privacy Policy

Inkognito for iPhone · Last updated 18 October 2026

Inkognito does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your best scores, Daily results, streak and settings are saved on your device. They never leave it, and the developer can't see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional Daily reminder, Inkognito asks iOS for permission and schedules a notification on your device. It is created and delivered locally, with no server involved. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Sharing results

When you tap Share, Inkognito passes a short text summary of your result to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Inkognito doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Network access

Inkognito works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Children

Inkognito collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
