Inkognito

A colour-and-word brain teaser for iPhone

How to play

Every card shows a colour word painted in a colour, and the two rarely match. A switch at the top tells you the rule: when it says INK, tap the colour of the paint. When it says WORD, tap the colour the word spells. The switch flips at random, so check it on every card. Faster answers score more.

Modes

Daily. 15 cards, the same for everyone, one go per day. Share your time and a grid of coloured squares: green is a fast correct answer, yellow a slower correct one, red a mistake. Each mistake adds 3 seconds.

Sprint. 60 seconds, as many cards as you can. Each mistake costs 2 seconds.

Arcade. 3 lives and a clock on every card. Each level adds one twist, such as more choices, WORD cards, and finally buttons painted in lying colours. Speed and streaks multiply your points.

Questions

When does the next Daily appear?
At midnight in your local time zone. The home screen shows a countdown.

Can I replay today's Daily?
No, there is one attempt per day so everyone's results are comparable.

Can I turn on a reminder?
Yes. Open Settings from the home screen and turn on Daily reminder. You choose the time, and it is skipped if you have already played that day.

Will my scores and streak move to a new phone?
Progress is stored on the device. It is included in iPhone backups, so restoring from a backup brings it back.

Does Inkognito need an internet connection?
No. It works fully offline.
